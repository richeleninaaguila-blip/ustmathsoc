A community built around mathematics.

The UST Mathematical Society is a student organization that provides opportunities for academic development, collaboration, leadership, and community engagement. Through its programs and initiatives, the organization aims to create meaningful experiences for students interested in mathematics and its applications.

UST DEPARTMENT OF MATHEMATICS AND PHYSICS

Excellence in mathematics and physics.

The Department of Mathematics and Physics is committed to providing exemplary programs in applied mathematics and physics through excellent student training, research, and community service.

01

Mission

The Department of Mathematics and Physics is committed to providing exemplary programs in applied mathematics and physics through excellent student training, research, and community service. The Department provides undergraduate students from various disciplines within the College of Science with a comprehensive yet innovative foundation in mathematics and physics that prepares them for analytical and critical scientific thinking.

02

Vision

The Department of Mathematics and Physics envisions itself as a Center of Excellence in Pure and Applied Mathematics and Physics, continuously providing high-quality training to students through effective instructional strategies and advanced research.
