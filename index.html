ABOUT MATHSOC

Mathematics as a key to growth.

The UST Mathematical Society-SCI is a recognized organization in the University of Santo Tomas that seeks to promote Mathematics as an important field while serving the Thomasian community.

Through academic assistance, student activities, leadership opportunities, and community initiatives, MathSoc provides its members with opportunities to grow professionally, morally, and socially.

UST MATHEMATICAL SOCIETY-SCI

Where limits do not exist.

01

Vision

The UST Mathematical Society-SCI envisions itself as a catalyst of academic excellence as it exposes Mathematics as an important field. It also envisions itself as the source of the professional, moral, and social formation of its members to become proud individuals who are preeminent in the field of Mathematics in this Catholic Institution.

02

Mission

The UST Mathematical Society-SCI, as a recognized organization in the University of Santo Tomas, aspires to serve the Thomasian community by providing mathematical assistance to students and promoting Mathematics as a key to intellectual development.

The organization, composed of individuals who are driven by their passion to share their knowledge in Mathematics in service to the Thomasian community, shall achieve its aspirations by upholding the Thomasian ideals and the teachings of the Church and believing in the importance of Compassion, Competence, and Commitment.
